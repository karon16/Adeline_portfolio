## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an editorial, contemplative, and poised aesthetic crafted for senior creative strategists, marketing directors, and entrepreneurial leaders. It merges the bespoke refinement of high-end literary editorial publications with the modern structure of progressive digital studios.

### Personality & Tone
- **Poised & Articulate:** Balanced authority through classical proportion and disciplined restraint.
- **Warm & Tactile:** Replaces sterile tech-neutrality with porcelain surfaces, sun-baked clay, and unhurried whitespace.
- **Intentional & Strategic:** Structured bento grids deliver content with clarity, avoiding decorative excess in favor of crisp typographic hierarchy.

### Design Movement
An intersection of **Warm Editorial Minimalism** and **Modern Tactile Architecture**. The interface uses asymmetric column spans, deep editorial italics, delicate hairline separations, and pill-shaped signifiers that communicate quiet confidence and intellectual rigor.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in warm organic tones, avoiding stark cold whites or synthetic pitch blacks.

### Color Architecture
- **Primary Canvas & Surfaces (`#FBF9F5`, `#F7F4EE`):** Warm porcelain and unbleached silk. Canvas backgrounds use `#FBF9F5`, while structural cards and layered modules rest upon `#F7F4EE`.
- **Text & Structure (`#1F1E1D`):** Rich espresso-charcoal. Provides supreme contrast without the harsh optical glare of `#000000`. Used for all key typography, crisp icons, and primary state fills.
- **Warm Taupe & Muted Greige (`#E8E2D8`):** Serves as secondary containment, subtle fill highlights, and divider structures.
- **Terracotta & Sun-dried Ochre (`#C56F5A`, `#D97E6A`):** The primary interactive accent. Signals active selections, pivotal metric highlights, focal links, and bespoke case study markers.
- **Muted Blush (`#E4B4A6`):** Soft contextual wash used for tag backgrounds, interactive hover states, and delicate visual emphasis.

### Border & Division Tokens
- **Hairline Border:** `rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.08)` for subtle card boundaries and section dividing lines.
- **Active Border:** `rgba(197, 111, 90, 0.40)` for focused inputs and highlighted credentials.

## Typography

The type system pairs **EB Garamond** for classical editorial voice and **DM Sans** for neutral, utilitarian operational balance.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Rules
- **Editorial Italics:** When featuring key narrative terms within `display-xl` and `headline-lg` (e.g., *Creative Strategy*, *Entrepreneurial*), use EB Garamond Italic to evoke magazine titling.
- **Labels & Badges:** `label-md` and `label-sm` must always be rendered uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to provide structural anchoring against the fluid serif headlines.
- **Body Copy Restraint:** Body paragraphs default to `body-md` in `rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.85)` rather than solid black to maintain page harmony.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on a relaxed 12-column editorial bento-grid, allowing generous breathing room around focal studies, case highlights, and biographical modules.

### Grid & Form Factor Specifications
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, max-width `1440px`, centered. Section margins at `4rem` (`margin`), column gutters at `1.5rem` (`gutter`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid. Margins compress to `2rem` (`margin-tablet`), gutters to `1.25rem` (`gutter-tablet`). Bento units collapse into paired 4-column blocks.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column fluid stack. Margins compress to `1.25rem` (`margin-mobile`), gutters to `1rem` (`gutter-mobile`). Bento units stack vertically in single-column succession.

### Rhythmic Padding Scale
- Use `space-xl` (56px) for generous inter-section boundaries.
- Use `space-lg` (32px) for inner padding of major bento containers.
- Use `space-md` (20px) for compact card interiors and grouped meta sections.
- Use `space-sm` (12px) and `space-xs` (6px) strictly for inline badge padding, icon gap sequences, and item list pairings.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal surface layering combined with diffused, warm-tinted ambient shadows and subtle hairline definition.

### Ambient Shadow Architecture
- **Resting Bento Card:** `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.03), 0 1px 3px rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.02);`
- **Interactive Card Hover:** `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -6px rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.07), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.03);`
- **Floating Modals & Overlays:** `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.12), 0 0 1px rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.15);`

### Hairline Borders & Inner Glows
Every elevated surface carries a delicate hairline border (`1px solid rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.08)`). Top-tier interactive cards leverage a soft inner rim highlight (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.60)`) to simulate the bevel of pressed paper or fine stationery.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, rounded aesthetic that offsets structural geometry with warmth.

### Radius System
- **Pill Elements (`rounded-full` / `9999px`):** Used for metadata tags, status indicators, filters, and standard call-to-action buttons.
- **Structural Containers (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem` to `2rem`):** Primary bento cards, project showcase showcases, and narrative text containers.
- **Form Controls & Inputs (`rounded-lg` / `1rem`):** Text fields, search bars, and selection controls.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Deep espresso (`#1F1E1D`) fill, porcelain (`#FBF9F5`) text, pill-shaped (`9999px`). Internal padding: `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Hover state introduces a smooth upward translation of `1px` and shifts background to `#C56F5A`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, `1px solid rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.18)`, text in `#1F1E1D`. Hover shifts border to `#1F1E1D` with background `rgba(232, 226, 216, 0.35)`.
- **Editorial Text Link:** Inline text with terracotta underline (`#C56F5A`) placed `4px` beneath text baseline, animating width from `0%` to `100%` on hover.

### Chips & Pill Badges
- **Status / Category Pill:** Background `#F7F4EE`, `1px solid rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.08)`, text `#1F1E1D`, rendered in `label-sm` (uppercase, tracked).
- **Accent Badge:** Background `rgba(228, 180, 166, 0.35)`, text `#C56F5A`, border `1px solid rgba(197, 111, 90, 0.20)`.

### Cards & Bento Modules
- **Foundation:** Background `#F7F4EE`, border `1px solid rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.08)`, border radius `1.5rem`, padding `2rem`.
- **Interactive State:** Hover elevates card via `transform: translateY(-3px)` with ambient shadow transition (`300ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
- **Featured / Hero Bento:** Inset inner highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`), subtle terracotta tag placement in upper-right corner.

### Form Inputs
- **Base Style:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.12)`, radius `0.75rem`, padding `0.875rem 1.25rem`. Text `body-md` in `#1F1E1D`.
- **Focus State:** Border color shifts to `#C56F5A` with an ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 111, 90, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes:** Size `1.25rem × 1.25rem`, radius `0.375rem`, border `1.5px solid rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.20)`. When checked, background becomes `#1F1E1D` with an off-white checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons:** Circular, outer ring matching checkbox borders, active inner disk colored in `#C56F5A`.

### Case Study / Portfolio List Row
- Linear editorial list view. Left side: publication year and client category (`label-sm`). Center: project title in `headline-sm` with EB Garamond Italic styling. Right side: floating arrow icon inside an `#E8E2D8` circular button that transitions to `#C56F5A` fill on hover.
